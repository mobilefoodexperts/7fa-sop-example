title: Retention — 30-Day Onboarding Sequence
description: The structured first 30 days of every new client engagement. Most churn is decided here — not in month 6.
---

## What

The first 30 days of a client engagement set every expectation that follows. Churn risk is highest in this window — not because results are slow, but because clients don't feel progress and stop trusting the agency. This SOP creates a structured, predictable first-30-days experience that replaces anxiety with forward motion. The goal is for every new client to feel, by Day 30, that signing was the right decision — even before big results have fully materialized.

**The rule:** Every client must experience a visible win before their first invoice cycle renews. If they can't point to something concrete, you are at risk.

## Who Owns It

**Account Manager** owns execution and all client communication. **Agency Owner** joins the Day 1 kickoff call and the Day 30 review. Nothing else in the first 30 days requires owner time — but those two touchpoints are non-negotiable.

---

## Steps

### Day 1 — Kickoff Call (60 Minutes)

Run within 48 hours of contract signature. This sets the tone for the entire engagement. Do not let a week pass before the first call.

**Agenda:**

| Time | Topic |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Welcome and relationship building — who are you, who are they, what does their day look like |
| 0:10–0:25 | Goals alignment — revisit what they said on the discovery call, confirm the 90-day target, get the 12-month vision on paper |
| 0:25–0:40 | Access collection — walk through the access checklist live; get logins and permissions in real time, don't chase them for two weeks |
| 0:40–0:50 | 30-day plan walkthrough — show them exactly what happens in the first 30 days, week by week; remove the black box |
| 0:50–0:60 | Standing meeting time — lock in a recurring weekly or bi-weekly call slot; if you don't set it now, scheduling becomes a friction point |

**By end of Day 1, you must have:**
- [ ] All critical access collected (GBP, GA4, website, CRM, ad accounts)
- [ ] 30-day plan document sent to the client
- [ ] Standing meeting on both calendars
- [ ] Welcome email sent with the plan summary and their Account Manager's direct contact

---

### Week 1 (Days 2–7) — Foundation Audit

Do the work immediately. Clients who don't see activity in the first week start to wonder what they paid for.

**Deliverables in Week 1:**

- **Baseline audit:** Document current state of every channel you'll be working on. GBP completeness score, website speed and mobile score, current keyword rankings (if SEO is in scope), current ad account structure and waste (if PPC is in scope), review count and recency.
- **Baseline report:** Send a simple 1-page document: "Here's where you are today." This becomes the before photo for the case study.
- **Quick fix list:** Identify 3–5 things you can fix in the first 7 days without waiting for strategy or approvals. GBP primary category wrong? Fix it. Missing service descriptions? Write them. No Google review link in email signature? Add it. Ship these immediately.
- **90-day roadmap:** Build a Notion/GHL/ClickUp dashboard the client can see. Every major milestone with expected date. No surprises.

---

### Week 2–3 (Days 8–21) — First Quick Win

The first tangible result must land before the end of Week 3. What counts as a quick win depends on the service:

| Service in Scope | Quick Win Example |
|---|---|
| Local SEO / GBP | GBP fully optimized + review system live + first 2–3 new reviews posted |
| Google Ads | First campaign live, first call or form fill tracked |
| Website | Site updated with new copy / landing page live |
| Content | First blog post published, GBP post live |
| Full stack | GBP optimized + one ad campaign live + baseline ranking report delivered |

**How to communicate the quick win:**

Don't wait for the standing call. Send a message the day it happens:

> "Quick update — your Google Business Profile is now fully optimized and the review request system is live. Your field team can start using the QR cards immediately. We'll watch for the first reviews to come in over the next 2 weeks."

Proactive updates = client confidence. Silence = anxiety.

---

### Day 30 — Review Call (45 Minutes)

This call is about proving forward motion and locking in the next 90 days.

**Agenda:**

| Time | Topic |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Wins recap — show the baseline vs. now side-by-side. Even small movements matter: "You went from 12 to 19 reviews. Your GBP completeness score went from 60% to 95%. Your site speed improved from 3.8s to 1.9s." |
| 0:10–0:25 | 90-day plan review — walk through what's coming in months 2 and 3; make sure expectations are calibrated ("SEO results typically become visible at 60–90 days — here's what we'll be tracking") |
| 0:25–0:35 | Feedback ask — "What's working so far? What could we do better? Anything unclear?" Get this on the record. |
| 0:35–0:45 | Referral ask — "By the way — is there anyone else in your network in [niche] who you think could benefit from what we're doing? We only take [X] new clients per month and we find most of our best relationships come through referrals." |

**Day 30 success criteria:**
- Client can articulate at least one concrete improvement since signing
- Client feels informed about what's coming in months 2 and 3
- No unresolved concerns or confusion going into month 2

---

### Days 31+ — Transition to Standing Cadence

After Day 30, the client moves onto the standard ongoing rhythm:

- Weekly or bi-weekly standing call (per their tier)
- Weekly report delivered on a consistent day (set the day on the kickoff call — never change it)
- Monthly dashboard updated
- Quarterly business review at 90 days

The first QBR at Day 90 is the real retention checkpoint. If the client is still skeptical at Day 90, address it directly on that call — do not let it drift to Day 120.

---

## Definition of Done

- Kickoff call completed within 48 hours of signature
- All critical access collected by end of Day 1
- Baseline audit report delivered by end of Day 7
- At least one quick win delivered and communicated by end of Day 21
- Day 30 review call completed with feedback captured
- Client moved to standing monthly cadence with all calendar holds in place
- First-90-day churn rate tracked monthly — target under 5%
