## What

A structured cold outreach sequence built around a personalized Google Business Profile audit delivered by Loom video. This is the primary lead generation motion for a niche agency with no ad budget — you build a list of 20–30 qualified prospects per month, audit their GBP and online presence, record a 3-minute Loom showing exactly what's broken, and send it with a soft call booking link. Response rates outperform generic cold email by 4–6x because the audit is specific and immediately useful to the recipient whether they hire you or not.

**When to run it:** Month 1 onward. This is your first revenue engine before paid ads, before SEO traffic, before referrals compound.

## Who Owns It

**Owner / Business Development** — do not delegate this until you have a proven script, a 10%+ reply rate, and a repeatable close rate. The founder's voice on a Loom converts significantly better than a VA's during the first 20 clients.

## Steps

### Step 1 — Build a Qualified Prospect List (30 per month)

Your list is the foundation. Garbage in, garbage out.

**Sources by niche:**
- Niche association directories (e.g., NAFED, ACCA, PHCC — find your industry's)
- Google Maps search: `[your niche service] near [city]` — scroll through the map pack and organic results
- Apollo.io filtered by NAICS code + revenue range + geography
- LinkedIn company search by industry + employee count

**Qualification filters — only add a prospect if:**
- They operate in your target niche (no generalists)
- Revenue range is within your serviceable bracket (use employee count and review volume as proxies if revenue isn't public)
- They have a Google Business Profile (required for the audit hook)
- They are NOT a current client and NOT a known competitor's locked client

**Score each prospect before outreach:**

| Signal | Green (Prioritize) | Yellow (Pursue) | Red (Skip) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google reviews | Under 20 reviews | 20–50 reviews | 200+ reviews (already winning) |
| GBP photos | Missing or outdated | Few, low quality | Professional, recent |
| Website | None or 5+ years old | Basic, no tracking | Modern with clear CTAs |
| Review recency | Last review 90+ days ago | Last review 30–90 days ago | Active weekly reviews |

Build in a spreadsheet: columns for Business Name, Owner Name (if findable), Phone, Email, GBP Link, Score (1–3), Status.

---

### Step 2 — Run the GBP Audit (10 minutes per prospect)

Before recording the Loom, do a real audit. You'll call out specific findings — this is what makes the video convert.

**Audit checklist:**

- [ ] GBP claimed and verified?
- [ ] Primary category correct?
- [ ] Business description filled in with relevant keywords?
- [ ] Services section complete with descriptions?
- [ ] Q&A section populated?
- [ ] Photos: minimum 10? Recent (within 90 days)? Owner photo present?
- [ ] Review count vs. top 3 local competitors?
- [ ] Last review response date?
- [ ] Website link correct and working?
- [ ] Posts section active (last 30 days)?

Note 2–3 specific, fixable findings per prospect. These become your Loom talking points.

---

### Step 3 — Record the Loom (3 minutes max)

**Structure — do not deviate:**

| Segment | Duration | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:20 | "Hi [First Name], I searched for [their service type] in [their city] and found your business — I'm a [your niche] marketing specialist and I noticed a few things on your Google profile that are likely costing you leads…" |
| Audit findings | 0:20–2:00 | Screen-share their GBP. Walk through 2–3 specific findings. Be direct. "Your last review is from 8 months ago — to Google, that signals inactivity." "Your primary category is set to X but you should also have Y and Z as secondary categories." |
| Soft close | 2:00–2:45 | "I put together a quick summary of what I'd fix first. Happy to walk you through a full audit on a 30-minute call — no pitch, just the findings. Link to book is below." |
| CTA | 2:45–3:00 | Show booking link on screen. "No obligation — if it's helpful, great. If not, at least you have the audit." |

**Recording rules:**
- Face cam ON — no exceptions. Anonymous Looms have sub-1% reply rates.
- Use their first name in the subject line and opening sentence — personalization is the hook.
- Keep it under 3 minutes. Anything longer gets abandoned.
- Do not use a template script verbatim — reference the audit notes and speak naturally.

---

### Step 4 — Send the Outreach Email

**Subject line formula:** `[First Name] — quick audit of [Business Name]'s Google profile`

**Email body:**

```
Hi [First Name],

I searched for [niche service] in [city] and came across [Business Name].

I recorded a quick 3-minute audit of your Google Business Profile — I found a few things that are likely affecting where you show up in local search.

[LOOM THUMBNAIL LINKED TO VIDEO]

If it's useful, I'm happy to do a full walkthrough on a 30-minute call. No pitch — just the findings and what I'd prioritize.

→ Book a time here: [CALENDAR LINK]

— [YOUR NAME]
[Agency Name] | [Niche]-focused digital marketing
[Phone] | [Website]
```

**Formatting rules:**
- Plain text, no HTML formatting, no logo headers — these look like mass emails and get filtered
- Loom thumbnail image linked directly to the video (not embedded)
- One CTA only — the calendar link
- No "I hope this email finds you well." No "I wanted to reach out." Start with value.

---

### Step 5 — Follow-Up Sequence (if no reply)

Most responses come after the second or third touch. Do not give up after one email.

| Day | Touch | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Loom email | Send the audit video |
| Day 4 | Email follow-up | "Quick follow-up — did you get a chance to watch the audit? Happy to answer any questions." (2 sentences max) |
| Day 8 | LinkedIn connection | Connect with a note: "Sent you a GBP audit for [Business Name] — thought it might be useful." |
| Day 12 | Email follow-up | Share one quick win from a client in their vertical: "Helped [similar business type] in [nearby city] go from 12 to 67 reviews in 90 days — happy to share how if it's relevant to where you're at." |
| Day 18 | Break-up email | "I'll stop following up after this — just wanted to make sure the audit got to you. If timing is ever right, calendar link is always open: [LINK]" |

**Hard rules:**
- Never follow up more than 5 times total
- Always log every touch in your CRM the same day
- If they reply "not interested" — remove immediately, no further contact, no question-asking

---

### Step 6 — CRM Logging

After every touch, update the prospect record:

- [ ] Touch date and channel logged
- [ ] Email open tracked (use a tracker like HubSpot free, Streak, or Instantly)
- [ ] Reply logged verbatim if received
- [ ] Status updated: Contacted → Opened → Replied → Call Booked → No Response → Disqualified
- [ ] Follow-up task set with specific date

---

## Definition of Done

- 20–30 new prospects worked per month
- Loom recorded and sent within 48 hours of prospect being scored
- Full 5-touch sequence completed for every non-responder
- CRM updated same-day after every touch
- Monthly metrics tracked: contacts worked, open rate, reply rate, calls booked, proposals sent, closes
- **Target benchmarks:** 10%+ reply rate, 4–5 strategy calls booked per 30 prospects
