## What

Positioning is the one decision that multiplies everything else. A generalist agency competes on price. A niche agency with clear positioning competes on authority. This SOP covers how to define your positioning, design a three-tier offer stack, write a converting homepage headline, and validate that it's working. Run this once at launch and revisit annually — or any time your close rate drops below 20%.

**The 7FA model in one sentence:** Pick a niche, become the obvious expert in it, charge what a specialist charges.

## Who Owns It

**Agency Owner** — this is never delegated. Your positioning is your strategy. No one else can decide this for you.

---

## Steps

### Step 1 — Define Your Niche (Non-Negotiable)

Generalist agencies do not scale past $300K/year without a team and significant ad spend. Niche agencies scale because every piece of content, every case study, every cold outreach, and every referral compounds within a small, knowable market.

**The niche selection criteria:**

| Criterion | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| You have genuine expertise or access | You can't fake authority in a niche — the buyers know the terminology, the real problems, the vendors |
| The niche has 2,000–10,000 businesses in the US | Small enough to dominate, large enough to build a real agency |
| Businesses in this niche can afford $1,500–$3,500/month | Trades, professional services, specialty retail, niche B2B |
| Search intent is local (they need customers in a geography) | Local SEO + Google Ads work reliably here |
| No large agency has clearly dominated the niche yet | Google "[niche] marketing agency" — if results are all generalists, there's a gap |

**The unfair advantage question:** Do you own, operate, or have deep history in a business in this niche? If yes — that is your primary positioning asset. No competitor can claim what you've actually lived.

---

### Step 2 — Write Your Positioning Statement

Fill in this template:

> **[Agency Name] is the only marketing agency that [specific differentiator] for [specific niche], which means [specific outcome clients get].**

**Examples of weak vs. strong positioning:**

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "We help businesses grow with digital marketing." | "We do marketing exclusively for fire protection companies — we own one." |
| "Results-driven agency for local businesses." | "The only marketing agency built for HVAC contractors, by an HVAC contractor." |
| "Full-service digital marketing for home services." | "We've generated 10,000+ leads for roofing companies across 12 states." |

**The test:** Can your prospect find a competitor who says exactly the same thing? If yes, you're not positioned — you're undifferentiated.

---

### Step 3 — Lock Your Homepage H1 Headline

Your H1 is your positioning compressed into one line. Write it before building the website. Do not use bravado claims or outcome promises — use specificity.

**Formula:** `[Service] built for [Niche]`

**Examples:**
- "Digital Marketing Built for Fire Protection Companies"
- "SEO and Ads Exclusively for HVAC Contractors"
- "Local Marketing for Independent Pest Control Companies"

**What goes below the H1 (subheadline):** Your differentiator.
- "Built by someone who actually owns a [niche] business — not a generalist agency."
- "We've worked with [X] [niche] companies across [X] states."

**What does NOT go in the hero:** Pricing, package names, or bold outcome claims. Those belong below the fold, supported by proof.

---

### Step 4 — Design the Three-Tier Offer Stack

Three tiers serve three purposes:
1. **Starter tier** — captures businesses not ready for the full program. Gets them in the ecosystem.
2. **Growth tier** — the most common close. Right-priced for a business that's proven the model and is ready to scale.
3. **Dominate tier** — anchors the price conversation upward and serves your best clients.

**Tier design rules:**
- Each tier builds on the previous (not a different product — a deeper version)
- Name the tiers for where the client is, not what they get
- Price with a gap: Starter → Growth should feel like a meaningful step up, not $100/month difference
- Month-to-month on all tiers — remove the objection before it's raised

**Tier design template:**

| | Starter | Growth | Dominate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Target client** | Solo / under $[X]K/yr | Small team / $[X]K–$[X]K/yr | Established / $[X]K–$[X]M/yr |
| **Monthly price** | $[X]/mo | $[X]/mo | $[X]/mo |
| **Core deliverables** | Foundation only | Foundation + lead gen channels | Full stack |
| **Calls** | 1 × 30 min/mo | 2 × 30 min/mo + QBR | Weekly + QBR |
| **90-day outcome** | Rankings + reviews baseline | Lead volume 2x | Market dominance in territory |

**Off-menu tier (never on the website):**
A stripped-down entry tier for prospects who are the right fit but genuinely cannot start at Starter pricing. Month-to-month. Offered only on a sales call, at your discretion. Never advertised — if it's on the website it becomes the expected starting price.

---

### Step 5 — Build the Authority Stack

Positioning is a claim. Authority is the proof. You need at least three of the following five before your first sales call:

| Asset | How to Get It Fast |
|---|---|
| **Case Study #1** | Use your own [niche] business if you own/operate one. Document from Day 1. Month 1, 3, and 6 stats. |
| **Credential or certification** | What does your niche respect? NFPA, ACCA, PHCC, manufacturer cert — get the one that signals legitimacy to your buyers. |
| **Podcast or YouTube channel** | 5 episodes minimum before launching. Niche-specific title. Consistency > production quality. |
| **Lead magnet** | A free PDF guide on the #1 problem in your niche. Gate it with email. Drives 20–30 qualified contacts per month with SEO. |
| **Association membership** | Join the niche trade association. Get listed in their directory. Attend one event. |

**The unfair advantage play:** If you operate a business in the niche you serve, that business becomes Case Study #1. Document every lead, ranking, and revenue dollar from Day 1. No competitor can replicate lived experience.

---

### Step 6 — Validate the Positioning (30-Day Test)

Before declaring the positioning locked, run these three tests:

1. **The cold Loom test:** Send 20 GBP audit Looms to niche prospects. What reply rate do you get? Under 5% = messaging is off. 10%+ = you have something.
2. **The sales call test:** Run 5 discovery calls. How many times do prospects say "I've been looking for someone who specializes in this"? If you hear it 3+ out of 5, the niche resonates.
3. **The referral test:** After your first 3 clients, do any of them refer a contact without being asked? Organic referrals in a niche = your positioning is doing the work.

If tests fail, go back to Step 1 and tighten the niche or the differentiator. Do not add budget or volume — fix the message first.

---

## Definition of Done

- Positioning statement written and committed to (not subject to weekly revision)
- H1 headline locked on the website
- Three-tier offer stack priced, named, and described on the /programs page
- At least three authority assets built or in progress
- 30-day validation test run with documented results
- Close rate on discovery calls is 20%+
